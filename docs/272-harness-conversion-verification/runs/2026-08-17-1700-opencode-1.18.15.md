## Findings

1. **planning#432** (filed from this run): OpenCode's lowercase tool
   vocabulary misses every name-keyed recognition registry, so to-do
   writes lose their data on the wire and render nothing, edits/writes
   render without paths or diffs, and subagent spawns lose their card and
   report. Layer A (adapter conversion) and the advertised-list guard are
   both green while this happens — a live demonstration that the
   name-list guard cannot see Layer B, which is the recipe's founding
   claim. The two surviving surfaces survive because their projection
   rules are key-based — the rename-immune pattern the fix should
   generalize.
2. The `bash`/`grep` survivals also localize the fix decision: either the
   opencode adapter normalizes names to the registry spellings, or the
   registries learn the opencode vocabulary (the recipe's registry
   appendix is the site checklist). Design call recorded in planning#432,
   not made here.
