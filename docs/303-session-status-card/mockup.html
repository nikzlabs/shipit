Session status card — freshness

docs/303 req 14: the state is carried by appearance only, never by title text. A current card is a regular card. A card that may be behind — after a question turn that skipped the update, or after an ignored nudge — carries a small "Stale" label in its bottom-right corner, in the theme accent (--color-accent). Below the two fields sit the agent's offered follow-up actions (req 16–18): every relevant one, persisting across turns, ticked and sent once; a taken one stays greyed out and unselected until the agent removes it (req 17). In place above the composer, on a light and a dark theme.
