Session status card — louder (round 2)

Round 1's verdict: “in all cases the view is very similar to other cards in the transcript. It needs to attract attention.” So these stop trying to be a well-behaved card. The neighbourhood is the real one this time: the agent's own prose and tool rows above, the composer directly below — the PR lifecycle card is pinned at the top of the session, far away, so it is not what the card competes with. First board is claude-light, the theme you use. Everything the card says is unchanged: same sections, same rows, same accent “Stale” label bottom-right.

  • E · Tinted — the whole surface in --color-accent-subtle with a full accent border. Nothing else in a conversation is tinted in the accent colour, so it is the one coloured object on screen. ← recommended
  • F · Capped — an accent-filled header strip across the top, the way a titled panel announces itself. Loud, and unmistakably not a message — but it adds a wordmark the card does not have today, so it is a requirement change (req 14 says no title text; today a current card looks like a regular card).
  • G · Band — it stops being a card: it breaks the conversation column edge to edge, loses its corners, and is bounded by a 2 px accent rule top and bottom. A band of UI, not a message.
  • H · Bigger — no colour move at all: wider padding, one type size up, a 12 px radius and a real shadow, so it is simply the largest object at the end of the conversation.
  • I · Max — E + G + H together: tinted, full-bleed, one size up. The loudest this card can be inside the token set.
