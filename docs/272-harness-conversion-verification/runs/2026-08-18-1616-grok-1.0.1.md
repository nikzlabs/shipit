## Two honest notes

**The subagent ran in BACKGROUND on this turn**, which the baseline's turn did
not do (that one completed in the foreground). So the report panel holds the
CLI's launch acknowledgement — "Subagent started in background. subagent_id:
…" — rendered as readable text rather than the raw `{"type":"Text",…}`
envelope the baseline would have shown. The count itself arrived through
`get_command_or_subagent_output`, whose result the model reproduced in prose
and which renders as a **raw-name row** by design: it is one of the six
meta/media tools `GROK_TOOL_MAP` deliberately leaves unmapped. Both envelope
shapes (`SubagentCompleted.output`, `Text.text`) are unwrapped by the
normalizer and both are covered by the fixture-replay guards, so the
foreground path is verified offline against the baseline's own capture even
though this turn happened to take the background one.

**Not driven, per the recipe's standing rule:** the interactive rows (user
question, plan mode) — headless cannot reach them. Those three tools
(`ask_user_question`, `enter_plan_mode`, `exit_plan_mode`) are exactly the
ones the normalizer deliberately leaves raw until their card-input shapes are
captured; see planning#437 and planning#435.
