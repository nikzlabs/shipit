Session status card — the look

The card as it ships (req 33): three capped cards rather than one, stacked Status · Last turn · Next steps. The two that are read take a soft cap — an accent tint with accent text — and the one that asks something takes the filled cap and goes last, nearest the composer, because it carries the Submit. Drawn in the card's real place — agent prose and tool rows above, the composer below — in four themes. The stack is the one coloured object in a conversation, which is what makes it findable at a glance without the session needing attention (req 9).

Where “Stale” goes. It describes the whole stack, not one card, so it sits at the right-hand end of the Status cap — the first thing read. On a stale stack the Last turn card is absent entirely (req 31 hides that line when the card may be behind).
