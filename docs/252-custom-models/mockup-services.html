Services as an add-flow — variant B

An alternative to the catalogue layout in mockup.html. There is no “Available” block and no grouping by service: you start empty, press Add service, and each thing you add becomes its own row. The catalogue still exists — it is what the dialog offers — but it is only visible while you are adding. Interactive: add and remove things.

Each row is one credential. A service can appear more than once — a subscription and an API key are different things to add.

What the picker sees

Derived, not configured. Rows collapse to one group per service × billing mode — several subscriptions to one service become a single group, because ShipIt routes between them (req 12).
req 7The catalogue moves into the dialog. Variant A shows every service ShipIt knows about in an “Available” block, so the screen grows with the catalogue whether or not you use any of it. Here the steady-state screen is only what you configured, and the catalogue appears at the moment it is a choice.
req 5Billing mode is picked at add time, not discovered later. Step 2 of the dialog is exactly the distinction the model picker groups on — so the thing the user chooses here is the thing they select there. In variant A the two modes are nested inside one service card, which reads as one thing that happens to have two halves.
req 15Discoverability is the cost. “Which services does ShipIt support?” is answerable at a glance in variant A and requires opening a dialog here. That matters more than usual because req 15 makes the shipped catalogue a promise — carrying OpenRouter and Vercel is only worth something if people find them.
openRows are credentials; picker groups are modes. Add two Anthropic subscription accounts and you get two rows but one picker group. That is correct — req 12 routes between them and the picker must not ask you to choose — but the two surfaces now count differently, and the panel on the right exists to make that legible rather than surprising. Worth deciding whether that is acceptable or whether accounts should nest inside one row, as variant A does.
