## Guards added (so this cannot silently regress)

- `opencode/opencode-tool-normalizer.test.ts` — every advertised
  OpenCode tool must map; every mapping must preserve canonical meaning
  (`canonicalizeTool("opencode", raw) === canonicalizeTool("claude",
  mapped)` — the canonical map as semantic oracle); and the mapped names
  are asserted against the REAL registries, including the two
  data-destroying defaults the recipe names: `inputKeyTreatment`'s `drop`
  (todos must be `keep`) and `rendersResultContentInline` (the subagent
  report must survive).
- `orchestrator/integration_tests/opencode-recognition-projection.test.ts`
  — a normalized edit/write must come out of `projectToolUse` with
  `diffStats` and its `file_path` (DIFF_INPUT_TOOLS is
  orchestrator-private, so this guard lives at the sanctioned layer
  crossing).
- `opencode/adapter.test.ts` — the real captured `write` line must map to
  `Write` + `file_path` at the adapter boundary.
