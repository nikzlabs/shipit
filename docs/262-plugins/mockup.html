C · Plugin card states — degraded and collision (reqs 13, 15, 20)

Card states, not transcript cards. Degraded distinguishes a failed refresh (the prior complete generation stays active — req 15) from a repo that never fetched (the session degrades visibly and continues — req 13). A collision names its domain and the exact declaration fix (req 20).
