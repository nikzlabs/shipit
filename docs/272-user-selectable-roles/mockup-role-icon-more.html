Role icon — more options

Twelve further candidates. Eight are stock Phosphor glyphs (solid border, green tag) and need no rule change; four are drawn (dashed border, amber tag) and exist only where Phosphor ships no equivalent — checked, rather than assumed: BaseballCap, Stamp, Ticket, Shapes, Intersect, CirclesThree, Selection and BracketsCurly all already exist, so drawing any of those would be pure cost. Every mark is shown at 30 / 20 / 16 / 13px, in the settings menu beside its real neighbours, and on the chip.

baseline Phosphor BriefcaseIcon — still the one to beat.
