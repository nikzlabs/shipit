  row; the two disagree exactly when a renderer swallows content.

## Findings

1. **planning#432 is fixed**, end to end, on a fresh session: names and
   keys normalize at the adapter boundary, every originally-failing
   surface gets its dedicated artifact, and everything survives reload.
2. **NEW — planning#434** (filed from this run): the subagent
   final-report body renders empty. OpenCode's `task` result wraps the
   report in `<task id=…><task_result>…</task_result></task>`; the
   content persists whole and reaches `SubagentReport`, but
   `MarkdownContent` renders with `skipHtml` (`message-markdown.tsx`),
   and the wrapper — one blank-line-free CommonMark HTML block — is
   dropped in its entirety, inner text included. The count survived this
   turn only because the model repeated it in prose. Fix directions
   (adapter-side unwrap vs client-side parse) recorded on the issue; not
   patched here — a verification run records, it does not patch.
3. Recurring, known, out of scope: session naming failed on Anthropic in
   the inner instance (`claude -p` spawn failure card) — planning#358
   class, also seen in both prior OpenCode runs; does not affect the
   matrix.
