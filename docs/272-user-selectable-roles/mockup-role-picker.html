User-selectable roles — picker prototype

Prototype for docs/272-user-selectable-roles. A selected role shows its name alone — the harness, model and reasoning level are what the role is made of, and restating them says nothing the user did not just decide (req 5). The name opens the roles, as every other control opens what it chooses between (req 14); the replaced controls come back from inside that list, and changing one is the whole of leaving the role (req 15). The name reports the user's choice — setting the same three values by hand never names a role (req 13). Roles are not in the model menu: a role is not a model, and that list is long already (req 16). Colours are the real claude-light and claude tokens; the composer is drawn from MessageInput.tsx. The role mark is Phosphor's BaseballCapIcon — chosen 2026-08-17 for the idiom “which hat you're wearing”, over BriefcaseIcon and a set of drawn marks.

The collapse is what pays for the control. docs/261 decided the composer row does not grow, and req 5 sets that aside deliberately. It is affordable because the growth is only in the state where no role is selected: choosing one removes three labels and adds one. The width problem that dominated the previous draft — the min-w-0 overflow-hidden clip group whose labels are cut between 700 and ~808px — is not made worse by a role, and is relieved while one is selected.

The mark alone, and only for someone who has a role (req 16). No label, no caret — matching the three bare controls at the left of the row, which also open menus without one. A user with no roles sees today's composer exactly, so the feature costs nothing until it is wanted. And the mark is learned in Settings, where it appears beside the word "Roles", so nobody meets it bare without having met it labelled. Neither half stands alone: an unlabelled mark nobody has seen is a puzzle, and using it in only one of the two places teaches nothing.
